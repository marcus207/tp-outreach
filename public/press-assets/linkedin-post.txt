£19m to deliver new Special Education Needs schools across the Midlands and the South

Turning Point Capital Advisory has arranged a £19m development and bridging facility for a specialist developer and operator in the Special Education Needs (SEN) sector, funding three new SEN schools across the Midlands and the South against a £47m turnkey value, a 40% loan to value.

The structure blends development finance for one school under construction with a bridging loan for the two completed schools ahead of a longer term refinance. It came together over a 24 month term, with six firms of solicitors working across two jurisdictions. Completing against the backdrop of February's white paper only further vindicated the quality of our client, with two sets of valuers and the lender all noting the quality of the operator and the culture across the whole organisation.

Demand for specialist SEN places continues to outstrip supply. Getting this funded means more provision for the children and families who need it, and that is what made it worth doing.
